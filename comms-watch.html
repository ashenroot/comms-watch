Analog & GMRS repeaters near you Feedline Labs · relay required · nightly cache

RepeaterBook's export API returns 401 auth_missing regardless of key, and its own terms forbid the bulk/offline reuse this panel would need anyway (see Not Connected, below) — so analogue coverage here comes from Feedline Labs' free, keyless repeater directory instead. Your relay sweeps it nationwide once a night and serves the result instantly from its own cache. Coverage is real but currently thinner and more uneven than RepeaterBook's would be, especially outside populated corridors — treat an empty result as unproven absence, not confirmed silence.

Reference and utility stations

Continuous transmitters with known site and power — the honest propagation yardstick

Why a known transmitter beats a beacon network. WSPR's receiver population is volunteer and uneven, so fewer spots is ambiguous between “propagation closed” and “receivers went offline”. WWV never stops transmitting, so absence of signal is unambiguously a property of the path. This page cannot hear them for you — it lists them so the check is one tune away.

How to read this dashboard

What the states mean

Deviation, not threshold. A value is not called anomalous for crossing a fixed line. It is compared against its own recent history at the same time of day, using a median-based score that a single wild reading cannot drag around. A quiet network and a busy one are each judged against themselves.

What it will not do

Missing is never green. A feed that fails renders as unknown, not as zero and not as nominal. If a panel is blank it is because nothing was received, and it says so.
No local observer is not "band closed". The local propagation panel reads other operators' receivers in your grid square. On a band nobody near you is monitoring, it reports that nobody is listening — which is a very different statement from the band being dead.
It is not an alerting system. Nothing here pages anyone, and no feed carries a guarantee of timeliness. Treat it as situational awareness for people who already know the bands, not as a monitor of record.
